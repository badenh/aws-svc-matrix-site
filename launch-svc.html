Service-by-service history of regions added or removed since the first snapshot in our collection.

How this is calculated
  • Each day the collector takes a fresh snapshot of the SSM global-infrastructure parameters and compares it to the previous day. Added cells (service × region appearing for the first time) become add events; removed cells become remove events.
  • The first day in the archive is the baseline: nothing before it is known, so no events are recorded for that day. The feed begins at day 2.
  • Services here are sorted by their most recent event date (newest first). Expand a service to see its chronological history.
  • Use the filters to narrow by service name, action, or date range.
What this is not
  1. This is not an official AWS launch announcement feed. Events reflect when a service × region first appeared in the AWS SSM global-infrastructure data, which can lag or lead public announcements by hours or days.
  2. Only cells for which both the service and region existed in the snapshot are tracked. New services and new regions themselves appear as a flurry of adds across the day they are first observed.
  3. Only the AWS commercial partition is covered.
