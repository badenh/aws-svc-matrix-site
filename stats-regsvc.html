How many AWS services does each region host, as a fraction of the full service catalogue in the current snapshot?

How this is calculated
  • Completeness = services available in region ÷ total services in catalogue, expressed as a percentage.
  • This is a theoretical measure: it assumes every region could host every service. In practice AWS does not deploy all services in all regions, and some services are intentionally regional. A region at < 100% is not necessarily "incomplete" in any operational sense.
  • Default (commercial) regions appear in black; opt-in regions appear in red, consistent with the main matrix.
  • The three tabs show all regions, default-only, and opt-in-only subsets.
What this is not
  1. Service availability is based on AWS-provided data. A service being available in a region does not mean that all sub-services, features, or sub-features of that service are available in the region. Feature-level parity varies and is not captured here.
  2. Completeness is not a quality or suitability score. A high percentage does not imply a region is "better"; a low percentage does not imply it is "worse". Region selection depends on workload, latency, residency, and pricing — none of which this page measures.
  3. Only the AWS commercial partition is covered. GovCloud, China, European sovereign cloud, and isolated regions are excluded.
